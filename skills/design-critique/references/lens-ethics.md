# Lens: ethics and dark patterns

Evaluate manipulation, honesty, and user agency. Findings here are reputational and trust risks - severity floors at Major.

- **Forced continuity and roach motels.** Easy in, hard out: cancellation, downgrade, disconnect, and data export must be findable and comparable in effort to their opposites.
- **Confirmshaming and steering.** Decline options worded neutrally (no "No thanks, I like losing revenue"); visual weight may recommend but not hide the alternative; preselected checkboxes for consent-like choices are findings.
- **False urgency and scarcity.** Countdown timers, "only X left", or activity nudges must be true and verifiable; fabricated pressure = Blocker.
- **Misdirection in consequences.** Consequential actions (spend, delete, share, publish, grant access) state their effect plainly before commitment; burying cost or scope in fine print is a finding.
- **Data honesty.** Collection moments say what and why; defaults favor privacy; consent is not bundled with unrelated agreements.
- **Charts that lie.** Truncated bar axes, cherry-picked windows, dual axes engineered for a narrative - flag in any user-facing analytics.
- **Autonomy in AI surfaces.** AI actions attributed, reversible where feasible, never impersonating a human (pairs with ai-agentic-ux trust patterns).

Frame recommendations as trust-preserving alternatives that still meet the business goal - the fix is honest persuasion, not no persuasion.
